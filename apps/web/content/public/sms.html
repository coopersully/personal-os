nohmi SMS terms and enrollment

Optional text messages about the work you choose to manage in nohmi.

Effective date: October 7, 2026. The nohmi messaging program is operated by John Cooper Sullivan. These terms supplement the Terms of use and Privacy policy.

What you are signing up for

With your consent, nohmi uses Twilio to verify your mobile number and deliver conversational messages from agents you authorize, reminders, workspace updates, and questions about your tasks, calendar, mail, or finances. The program is for your own account and authorized work, not advertising or purchased contact lists. Available workflows depend on your connections and agent permissions; not every free-form text can be completed automatically.

Message frequency varies with your requests, activity, and settings. Message and data rates may apply. Texting is optional. Consent is not a condition of purchase or of using the rest of nohmi.

How to enroll

  1. Sign in and open Settings → Texting, or open the phone number controls in your profile.
  2. Enter a mobile number you control in the United States or Canada.
  3. Read the disclosures and select the unchecked “Allow agent text messages” checkbox.
  4. Choose “Send verification code.” Twilio sends an SMS code to that number.
  5. Enter the code and choose “Verify and connect.” nohmi records your consent and connects the verified number.

This public page explains the enrollment flow; it does not collect a phone number or enroll you. You can keep using nohmi without enrolling in texts. Simply creating an account or visiting this page does not give messaging consent.

The consent you see

I agree to receive verification codes and conversational agent texts, reminders, workspace updates, and questions from nohmi at the number I provide. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to unsubscribe or HELP for help. Consent is optional and is not a condition of purchase or of using nohmi.

The enrollment screen links to this page, the Privacy policy, and the Terms of use.

Stop, resume, and get help

Reply STOP to the nohmi program number to unsubscribe. You may receive a final opt-out confirmation. You can also disconnect your number in Settings → Texting. Reply HELP for help, or email coopersully02@gmail.com.

After a carrier or Twilio opt-out, reconnecting in the app alone does not unblock delivery. Reply START to the same program number to resume, provided your nohmi connection is still active. If you disconnected the number, enroll and verify it again. START alone does not create a nohmi account or connect a new number.

Delivery and privacy

Delivery depends on your carrier, device, and service availability. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages. SMS is not an emergency service and is not end-to-end encrypted. Messages may appear on a lock screen. Do not text sensitive credentials or full account numbers.

We record your phone number, verification and consent history, message contents, and delivery information to operate the program and honor opt-outs. Mobile information is not shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. SMS opt-in data and consent are not sold, rented, shared, or transferred to third parties or affiliates. Twilio and carriers process the information necessary to provide messaging. Read the Privacy policy for retention and data request details.
