Support and contact

Get help with nohmi, your account, or your data.

Contact the operator

nohmi is operated by John Cooper Sullivan. Email coopersully02@gmail.com for product support, account access, SMS help, privacy requests, or security reports. This is also the contact for questions about the Terms of use.

Help us understand the problem

Include your account email, the affected feature, what you expected, and the approximate time and time zone. If you share a screenshot, remove private messages, financial details, and credentials first. Never send passwords, verification codes, access tokens, or full payment or bank account numbers.

Text messages

Reply STOP to unsubscribe from program messages or HELP for help. You can also disconnect your phone in Settings → Texting. If messages continue or your phone number has changed, email us. See SMS terms and enrollment.

Privacy and account requests

State whether you want access, a correction, information about available exports, deletion of specific information, or account deletion. We may need to verify your identity before acting. See the Privacy policy for retention and request details.

Security reports

Email a concise description of the issue and how to reproduce it without including another person’s data. Do not access accounts you do not own, disrupt the service, or publish sensitive details while reporting a concern.

Urgent matters

nohmi and its support inbox are not emergency services. Use the appropriate emergency service for urgent threats to health or safety. No particular support response time is guaranteed.
