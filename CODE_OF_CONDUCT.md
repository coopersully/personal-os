# Code of conduct

## Participating

Be respectful in project issues, pull requests, discussions, and other project spaces. When you disagree, explain the effect of a proposed change and give the other person room to respond.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, disclosure of personal data, and misuse of another person’s account or integrations are unacceptable. Use synthetic or redacted examples when discussing mail, calendars, tasks, or financial records.

## Reporting a concern

Contact a project maintainer privately. Describe what happened and include a link to the relevant interaction if available. Keep identifying or sensitive details out of public threads. For a software vulnerability, use the [security reporting process](SECURITY.md).

Maintainers may remove content or restrict participation when someone violates this standard.
