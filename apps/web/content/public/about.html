About nohmi

Know what matters. Keep control of what happens next.

Our mission

nohmi exists to make the work of managing a life lighter: keeping commitments, communicating clearly, protecting time, and understanding money. It brings that work into one place where you can act directly and choose what to delegate.

Our aim is useful assistance that earns your trust through clear permissions, visible results, and room for your judgment. Your priorities should guide the software.

Four familiar workspaces

  • Mail: read and manage connected mail.
  • Tasks: organize tasks, reminders, and commitments.
  • Calendar: see and manage your time.
  • Finances: organize accounts, transactions, and budgets.

Connected services remain the source of their records. Available integrations and automation depend on your account, permissions, and the services you connect. Our mission guides the product’s development; automation support varies by workflow.

You and your authorized agents

You can connect an agent and grant access to specific kinds of work. Review its permissions and activity, and revoke access when you no longer want it. Optional text messages let authorized agents reach your verified phone number.

Start with the product guide, learn how data and agent access work, or read about nohmi text messages.
