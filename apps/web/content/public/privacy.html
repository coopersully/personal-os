Privacy policy

How nohmi collects, uses, and protects personal information.

Effective date: October 7, 2026. This policy covers nohmi operated by John Cooper Sullivan. Contact coopersully02@gmail.com for privacy questions or requests.

Information we collect

  • Account information: your email address, profile details, authentication records, preferences, and account security information.
  • Information you add: tasks, reminders, notes, goals, personal context, and other material you enter or ask an authorized agent to record.
  • Connected-service information: mail and associated metadata or attachments, calendar events, and financial account and transaction data, according to the services and permissions you authorize. Connection credentials and tokens support those integrations.
  • Texting information: your phone number and country, verification and consent records, message contents, timestamps, delivery status, and opt-out history.
  • Location and notifications: a location you select or allow your device to share for features such as weather, and notification subscription information if you enable notifications.
  • Operational information: request and security logs, session and device information, network information such as IP addresses, action history, and support correspondence.

How we use information

We use information to provide your workspaces, synchronize authorized connections, carry out requested actions, support agents you authorize, send requested communications, maintain security, diagnose problems, and respond to support and privacy requests. Information about your preferences and context helps support the work you choose to perform in nohmi.

Service providers and authorized recipients

Infrastructure and communication providers process information needed to operate nohmi. Twilio processes phone numbers, verification requests, SMS contents, and delivery information. Email delivery providers process account emails. Connected providers process the requests you authorize; financial connections may use Plaid. An agent or external AI service you connect can receive information within the access you grant and processes it under its own terms and privacy policy.

We may disclose information when required by law or when reasonably necessary to protect people, rights, and service security. Access for support and operations is limited to the purpose requiring it. We do not sell personal information or provide it for third-party targeted advertising.

Mobile information and SMS consent

Mobile information is not shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging opt-in data and consent are not sold, rented, transferred, or shared with third parties or affiliates, including lead generators. Consent is specific to nohmi and cannot be used to enroll you in another sender’s program. These restrictions apply to all sharing described in this policy.

Twilio and carriers necessarily process phone numbers and message information to verify your number, deliver texts, and enforce opt-outs; this does not authorize them to use your consent for their own marketing. See the SMS terms.

Cookies and local storage

nohmi uses session cookies and device storage for authentication, security, preferences, and application operation. The installed web app may cache application files. Clearing browser storage can sign you out or reset local preferences. These mechanisms are not used to sell your information or target third-party advertisements.

Retention and deletion

We retain account and workspace information as needed to provide the service and preserve requested history. Security, action, and consent records may be retained to investigate incidents, honor opt-outs, resolve disputes, and meet legal obligations. Backups and provider-held records may persist after removal from active systems until their retention periods expire. We do not promise immediate deletion from every backup or third-party system.

Disconnecting a provider, revoking an agent, or opting out of texts stops the relevant future access or messaging; it does not automatically erase existing records. Email coopersully02@gmail.com to request access, correction, an available export, or deletion of your information or account. We may need to verify your identity. We will explain applicable limitations, including records we need to retain. Rights and response requirements depend on where you live.

Security and international processing

nohmi uses access controls, account isolation, protected connections, and credential safeguards. No service can guarantee absolute security. SMS is not end-to-end encrypted and can appear on a shared device or lock screen. Information may be processed in the United States and other countries where our service providers operate.

Children

nohmi is intended for adults aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Contact us if you believe a child has provided information so we can investigate and take appropriate action.

Changes and contact

We will update this page and its effective date when the policy changes and provide additional notice where required. Questions and requests go to John Cooper Sullivan at coopersully02@gmail.com.
