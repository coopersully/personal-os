nohmi / about

a personal harness.

your context, tools, and controls. built around you.

built around a person

agents have harnesses: the context, tools, and controls that let them work. we’re building that kind of support around a person.

nohmi brings your mail, tasks, calendar, and money into one product. work directly, connect agents you choose, and decide what to delegate. your priorities guide the work.

familiar tools

  • mail: read, reply, and organize across connected inboxes.
  • tasks: keep commitments, projects, and next steps together.
  • calendar: see and manage your time across calendars.
  • finances: understand accounts, transactions, and budgets.

control stays with you

choose what to connect and what each agent can access. inspect proposed work and recorded changes. revoke access when you need to. available actions depend on the workflow, your permissions, and the services you connect.

this is our product direction. nohmi is in alpha, and capabilities are still developing. connected providers remain authoritative for their records.
