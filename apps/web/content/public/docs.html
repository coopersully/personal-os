nohmi / guide

set up your harness.

start with your work. add help when you need it.

get in

create an account with your invitation, or sign in. set your time zone and daily preferences.

connect your tools

connect supported mail, calendar, or financial accounts through the workspace’s connection controls. read the requested permissions and connect only what you need. access can expire; check connection health when records stop updating.

work directly

  • read and reply in mail.
  • capture commitments in tasks.
  • plan your time in calendar.
  • review accounts and spending in finances.

available actions depend on your account and connected services. a stale record or connection error is not confirmation that an external action succeeded.

bring an agent

use the connected-agent setup to authorize your chosen agent. give it a specific job and only the access it needs. inspect proposed actions and recorded results. you can revoke access at any time.

schedule recurring work in your agent host where supported. nohmi owns its records and permission checks; your chosen host invokes the work. see data and agent access before sharing sensitive context.

stay reachable

texting is optional. open settings → texting, read the disclosures, opt in, and verify your number. enrollment can also start from your profile. see texting terms and enrollment for consent, availability, and opt-out details.

get help

contact support with the affected feature and approximate time. never send passwords, verification codes, full account numbers, or private message contents.
