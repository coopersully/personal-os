today: decide what matters now

today brings relevant work and decisions together across the workspaces. use it to orient yourself, inspect work that needs attention, and open the owning workspace for detail. it is a shared starting point rather than another place to store duplicate tasks or events.

an agent can use authorized workspace context and attention items to help you prepare a daily plan. ask for the commitments, open questions, and conflicts that matter today. a summary is an aid to choosing; it does not itself complete a task, send a message, or change your calendar.
