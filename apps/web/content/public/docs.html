your tools. your terms.

work directly. delegate deliberately. keep the context together.

start with one workspace

nohmi is a personal harness: your context, tools, and controls in one place. it gives you familiar workspaces for communication, commitments, time, and money. you can use them yourself, connect an agent, or switch between both. the records belong to the workspace, so delegated work stays connected to what you see in the app.

  1. create an account with an invitation, or sign in. set your time zone and preferences.
  2. open the workspace you need first. connect a supported source through its connection controls, or create local records where available.
  3. check what has synced and when. read the permissions before connecting another account.
  4. try one manual workflow before delegating it. then give your agent a small, specific job with a clear stopping point.

nohmi is in alpha. this guide describes implemented workflows, not a promise that every provider, account, or deployment supports every operation. the controls, connection capabilities, and permissions shown in your account determine what is available. broader automatic stewardship remains a product direction, not a guarantee that all work happens unattended.
